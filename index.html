💡 Virtual Room Guidelines / මාර්ගෝපදේශ / வழிகாட்டுதல்கள்
English:
1. Please be mindful of your language and respectful while chatting, as seniors and fellow campus members are present in this virtual room.
2. Chat clear option and chat timer options remove messages from your device only, whereas the message delete option removes the message completely for everyone.
3. Avoid sensitive content such as inappropriate material, phone numbers, email addresses, and spam messages to prevent your account from being blocked.
4. We believe this virtual room helps you share educational information safely and cooperatively across the faculty. - Web Admin Team
